Underline Utilities

WispCSS supplements Tailwind's built-in underline with CJK offset adjustment and additional decoration styles. The .underline class adds text-underline-offset: .2em for lang="zh", lang="ja", and lang="ko" to prevent the underline from intersecting CJK descenders.

Notes

  • Use .underline for basic underline with CJK offset (replaces Tailwind's underline for CJK content).
  • Style variants: underline-wavy, underline-dashed, underline-dotted, underline-solid, underline-double.
  • CJK offset applies automatically when the element has lang="zh", lang="ja", or lang="ko".
